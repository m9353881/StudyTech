Study Techniques

Pomodoro Technique

The Pomodoro Technique divides study time into focused sessions followed by short breaks.

How to use it:

  1. Choose a task.
  2. Study for 25 minutes.
  3. Take a 5-minute break.
  4. Repeat the process.

Active Recall

Active Recall is a technique where you actively retrieve information from your memory.

How to use it:

  1. Read and understand the topic.
  2. Close your notes.
  3. Ask yourself questions.
  4. Answer from memory.

Blurting Technique

Blurting involves writing everything you remember about a topic without looking at your notes.

How to use it:

  1. Study a topic.
  2. Close your notes.
  3. Write everything you remember.
  4. Compare your answer with your notes.
